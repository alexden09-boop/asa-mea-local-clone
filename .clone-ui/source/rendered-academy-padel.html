OUR PADEL ACADEMY

Welcome to ASA Padel Academy

At ASA Padel Academy, one of Beirut's largest padel arenas, we're dedicated to helping you master padel technique. Our top-notch facilities and expert coaches create an ideal setting for players of all levels to enhance their skills.

We offer tailored programs for everyone, from beginners to advanced players, focusing on technique and strategy. Our experienced coaches provide personalized instruction to help you reach your full potential.

Join us at ASA Padel Academy to refine your padel game in a vibrant community. It's the perfect place for you to learn, improve, and enjoy the sport.

Our Padel Academy's objective is to prepare kids from a young age for the professionalism of the game. Beyond the social aspects and fun that the game offers, we focus on developing their fitness and logical skills. At ASA, we teach the kids the basics of the game and closely monitor their progress to introduce them to advanced techniques and new skills.

We are Mainly located in Tallet el Khayat and we will be soon opening in a new location. Our padel academy can accommodate between 50 to 80 kids per hour. We have 5 padel coaches, each responsible for their own court. classes are divided between age and level.
